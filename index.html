SEC.01 // Overview

Depth over speed.
Understanding over rushing.

PROJECT ETERNITY isn't about rushing through mods — it's about understanding them.

Rather than simply moving from one mod to another, you'll progress through a network of engineering disciplines, each introducing new scientific principles, technologies, resources, and custom machinery that expand upon what came before.

Every discipline is designed to feel like a genuine technological advancement — eventually taking you from conventional industry to systems capable of manipulating matter, energy, information, and forces far beyond ordinary automation.

MODS PROVIDE THE FOUNDATIONS. PROJECT ETERNITY PROVIDES THE PROGRESSION.
